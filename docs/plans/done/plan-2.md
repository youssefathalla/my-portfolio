Included on /policies to build agency-grade trust while protecting your time:

* **60-Day Post-Launch Bug Guarantee:** Free resolution for any scope-defined bug within 60 days of deployment.  
* **Fair-Play Scope Policy:**  
  * *Before Coding:* 100% free and flexible changes to requirements and wireframes.  
  * *Unbuilt Features:* Free swapping for a feature of equal engineering complexity.  
  * *Coded/Approved Features:* Changes are quoted transparently as a Change Order sprint.

* **Post-Warranty Care Plans:** Essential (3 Months), Growth (6 Months), and Enterprise (1 Year).
