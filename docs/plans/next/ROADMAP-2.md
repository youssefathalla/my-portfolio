## **1\. Post-Launch QA & SEO Verification (Immediate)**

Before driving traffic to the site, ensure all live production checks pass cleanly:

- 🔍 **Google Search Console & Sitemap:** Submit your live sitemap.xml URL (<https://youssefathalla.com/sitemap.xml>) to Google Search Console to index all your new sub-routes (/services/fixed-mvp, /policies, etc.).
- 📱 **Social Card Preview Validation:** Run your URLs through the [LinkedIn Post Inspector](https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/) and \[Twitter Card Validator\] to ensure OpenGraph metadata and social preview images render properly.
- 🧪 **End-to-End Test Form & Booking:** Perform a real test submission through your Formspree endpoint and verify that your Cal.com booking triggers (both 30-min discovery and 15-min urgent) successfully create calendar invites.
- ⚡ **Production Lighthouse Audit:** Run a live production audit to verify that prerendering (SSG) delivers $95+$ Performance and $100$ Accessibility scores across mobile and desktop viewports.
